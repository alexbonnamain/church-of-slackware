# ⛧ Church of Slackware ⛧ 🐧

*A tribute to Slackware Linux, the KISS philosophy, and the Unix spirit.*

## About This Project

Church of Slackware is a personal tribute to one of the oldest GNU/Linux distributions still actively maintained.

Slackware has played a very special role in my journey with GNU/Linux.

I have used Slackware for many years, and it was this distribution that inspired me to explore GNU/Linux more deeply, understand how the system works, and appreciate the philosophy behind it.

Slackware taught me the value of simplicity, independence, and understanding what happens beneath the surface.

Even today, I continue to use Slackware on my personal server.

This website is my way of expressing my appreciation and affection for a distribution that has meant so much to me.

The religious references throughout the website are purely satirical and intended as a lighthearted celebration of Slackware and its community.

## Philosophy

**KISS — Keep It Simple, Stupid!**

No frameworks. No unnecessary dependencies. Just HTML, CSS, and a little Unix humor.
